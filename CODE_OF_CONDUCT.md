# Code of conduct

Be respectful and specific. Welcome contributors regardless of experience, identity, nationality or background. Discuss ideas and behavior without personal attacks, harassment, discriminatory language, threats or disclosure of another person's private information. Respect consent when sharing screen captures and logs.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, limit participation or ban repeated offenders. Apply enforcement consistently and provide a short explanation when safe. Good-faith criticism and technical disagreement are welcome.

Use GitHub's [Report content](https://docs.github.com/en/community-and-safety-accessing-content/using-the-report-content-feature) control for abusive behavior on GitHub. For security reports, use the repository's [private vulnerability form](https://github.com/nishuod/dsh-plugin-virtual-desktop/security/advisories/new). Keep private incident details out of public issues.
